🧠 Cognitive Restructuring

Constant short-form media exposure fragments your baseline attention span and erodes deep-focus capabilities.

Trade-off: Weakens patience, critical thinking, and working memory performance.

Deep Dive: Switching tasks every few seconds conditions your brain to crave constant novelty. Rebuilding focus requires intentional blocks of offline time.

🌙 Circadian Disruption

Blue light emissions from digital displays suppress your brain's natural melatonin production late into the evening.

Trade-off: Delays sleep onset and blocks critical recovery phases.

Deep Dive: Poor sleep directly affects memory consolidation and physical energy. Set a 1-hour digital curfew before bed.

Click to expand ▾

⚡ Dopamine Desensitization

Endless algorithmic scrolling forces rapid, artificial dopamine spikes that hijack your biological reward circuitry.

Trade-off: Lowers baseline focus, leaving real-life tasks feeling boring.

Deep Dive: Overstimulated reward pathways make everyday tasks feel boring. Periodic detoxes help restore normal sensitivity.
