Why We Must Change Habits

🧠 Cognitive Restructuring

Constant short-form media exposure fragments your baseline attention span and erodes deep-focus capabilities.

Trade-off: Weakens patience, critical thinking, and working memory performance.

🌙 Circadian Disruption

Blue light emissions from digital displays suppress your brain's natural melatonin production late into the evening.

Trade-off: Delays sleep onset and blocks critical recovery phases.

⚡ Dopamine Desensitization

Endless algorithmic scrolling forces rapid, artificial dopamine spikes that hijack your brain’s biological reward circuitry.

Trade-off: Lowers baseline focus, leaving real-life tasks feeling boring.
